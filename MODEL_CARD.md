# GajaAlert AI - Model Card

## Model Overview

- **Model Name**: GajaAlert MobileNetV2 Elephant Detector
- **Architecture**: MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning with Custom Night Augmentation Head
- **Input Resolution**: 224 x 224 x 3 (RGB)
- **Output Classes**: `elephant` (0), `other_animal` (1), `no_animal` (2)
- **Format**: Keras SavedModel (`.keras`)
- **Explainability**: Grad-CAM target layer `out_relu` (last conv layer of MobileNetV2)

---

## Intended Use & Field Context

- **Primary Application**: Automated early warning intrusion detection at forest-village borders near Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.
- **Intended Users**: Forest Department officials, village task forces, local residents.
- **Out-of-Scope Uses**: Automated lethality triggers or autonomous physical barriers. GajaAlert AI provides advisory and alert notifications only.

---

## Technical Innovation: Custom `tf.data` Night Augmentation

To address night-time performance degradation in infrared and low-light camera traps, ~40% of training frames are dynamically augmented on-the-fly:
1. **Darkening**: Brightness multiplied by $U(0.15, 0.60)$
2. **IR Tinting**: RGB scaling factors $(0.85, 0.95, 1.10)$
3. **Gaussian Noise**: $\sigma = 8.0$ pixel standard deviation
4. **Range Clipping**: Values clamped to $[0, 255]$

Validation and test sets remain clean to measure generalization to dark environments accurately.

## Risk Logic & Operational Safety

In compliance with domain safety standards:
- Missing an elephant intrusion (False Negative) is significantly worse than a false alarm (False Positive).
- **Default Operational Threshold**: $0.60$. Any elephant detection with confidence $< 0.60$ flags `review_needed = True`.
- **Base Risk**: $0.85$ confidence triggers `MEDIUM` risk; below $0.85$ triggers `LOW` risk.
- **Context Escalation**: $+1$ level for night time (18:00 - 06:00), $+1$ level for repeat sighting within 30 minutes at the same site. Cap at `HIGH`.
